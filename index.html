THE IDEA · 01

Mud travels through the drillpipe, out through the bit, and back up the annulus with rock cuttings.

The circulation loop helps clean the hole and cool the bit. Flow rate, drilling-fluid properties and annular geometry all affect how effectively the fluid removes cuttings.
