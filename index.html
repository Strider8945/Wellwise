01 / 03 — FOLLOW THE MUD

Down the pipe. Up the annulus.

One fluid circuit. Two directions. See how mud moves through a well.

DOWNHOLE · NOT TO SCALE
● CIRCULATING
Mud downReturns upCuttings

Mud travels down the drillpipe, exits the bit nozzles, and returns up the annulus carrying cuttings.

Circulation also cools the bit and cleans the hole. Flow rate, drilling-fluid properties and geometry affect the transport of cuttings.
